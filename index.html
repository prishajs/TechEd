What does AI actually do?

Artificial intelligence is software that learns patterns from large amounts of data, then uses those patterns to make a prediction, a decision, or something new.

What AI can't do (yet)

  • It can be wrong, and sound sure. Always check important facts.
  • It reflects its training data. Gaps and biases in the data show up in the results.
  • It doesn't understand like a person. It finds patterns; it has no experiences or intentions.
